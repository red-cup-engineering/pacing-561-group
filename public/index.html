What this app is, and is not

Every number here describes how today compares to your own recent history — never a population norm, and never a diagnosis. Nothing this app shows predicts, confirms, or diagnoses post-exertional malaise; it flags conservative deviations from your own baseline and tells you, in plain language, what evidence stands behind each flag.

The math is published and open — you can read exactly how every number is computed and what its evidence status is, unlike the closed scoring formulas most pacing apps ship.
